🎯 About this website

Focus16 is a distraction‑free focus timer and study companion, built out of a very simple, personal need: Pushpraj wanted a better way to study and stay organized — so he built one.

It's not meant to be flashy. It's meant to be useful — a calm space with a timer, a todo list, and study materials, designed to make focusing feel a little less like a fight and studying feel a little less stressful.

It's also a work in progress, built step by step, the same way its creator likes to learn: by experimenting, improving, and getting a little better each time.
